SafeSend Moderator review

Moderator sign in

Enter the moderator password (MODERATOR_TOKEN in the server's .env).

Review queue

Appeals are messages the filter blocked that the sender thinks are fine. Reports are delivered messages that someone flagged.

AI check sensitivity

How sure the AI must be before it blocks a message. Only moderators can change this; users can't send blocked messages. Word-list matches are always blocked.

Always allow

Real names or words that look like bad words but aren't, for example a surname or a place. The word list will never block these words. The AI check still reads the whole message.
